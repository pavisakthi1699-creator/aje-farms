## House style followed

Matched against the 39 published lessons (179–376 chars):

- Swahili, practical, imperative.
- **No chemical/product names and no dosages.** The source pages *do* name
  products (Dimethroate, Melathion, Metalaxyl, Attakan C, Imidacloprid) — these
  are deliberately omitted, exactly as the published lessons omit them.
- Risky calls deferred: "fuata maelekezo ya mtaalamu", "thibitisha na afisa ugani".
- Money figures omitted (the passion-fruit source quotes TZS 4M/acre and
  3,000–5,000/kg, which date quickly).
